How this was made

The Guardian’s Storylines module chose this thread, and the articles in it, as one of the three strongest on the Manchester City topic page. An AI model (Claude) read the articles and designed this animation in code, using no words, dates or figures but theirs. The animation is written as a web page (see it play live) and rendered frame by frame to video; the music and sound effects are synthesised in code, with no samples or speech. Every piece of on-screen text is tied to a passage in the journalism: the full list is in the provenance manifest. This is an experiment and has not yet been checked by a Guardian editor.
